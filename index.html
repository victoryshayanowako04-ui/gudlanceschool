    How are profit, shortage, excess, and the next order estimated?

    Surplus / (loss) is allocated stationery payments received in the selected dates minus the recorded cost of items issued in those dates. Billed-but-unpaid fees, waivers, credits, and unallocated payments are excluded. Shortage compares forecast demand with on-hand stock plus open orders; excess is on-hand stock above forecast. Forecasts use average quantity per recipient across recorded academic periods and the current number of students marked as stationery users. It is an estimate, not an automatic order.
